# Realistic 3D human assets: pricing research (2026-11-13)

No purchases, paid API calls, generations, or project edits were made.

## Published API rates (Tripo)
- Tripo API pricing: https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/pricing
- Standard-textured text-to-3D: $0.20/model; auto-rigging: $0.25/model; animation retargeting: $0.10/preset animation.
- Illustrative baseline for two generated assets, each textured and rigged, plus one preset animation each: 2 x ($0.20 + $0.25 + $0.10) = $1.10, before tax/retries. This is not a complete quote for the requested custom assets or the Manus-integrated tool.
- Preset animation retargeting docs: https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/docs/animations-retarget . Published retarget endpoint covers preset animations; custom candle-lighting/blowing choreography and custom facial animation are not priced there.
- Tripo Studio plans: https://www.tripo3d.ai/pricing

## No-cost alternative
- Adobe Mixamo FAQ: https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html . Free with Adobe ID; FAQ permits royalty-free personal, commercial, and nonprofit use; humanoid biped limitation.
- Blender license: https://www.blender.org/about/license/ . Blender is free/open-source.
- Recommended no-purchase route: Mixamo humanoid character/body motion + Blender for custom pose/face/blow action and Three.js integration. Tradeoff: hands-on animation work, likely less photorealistic than the marketplace examples; need verify each source asset's license and web-ready formats.

Manus 3D generation tools are marked paid, but no per-operation cost was exposed in the available interface/docs; exact charge cannot be quoted. Custom action authoring and integration time/cost are also not published. Do not initiate any paid work without the user's separate approval.


## Follow-up: free-only feasibility
- Adobe Mixamo official FAQ: https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html . Free with Adobe ID/no subscription; character and animation use royalty-free for personal, commercial, and nonprofit projects; humanoid bipeds only.
- Reallusion CC Character Base free page: https://www.reallusion.com/character-creator/free-3d-character-base.html . Advertises 5 rigged FBX/OBJ/ZTL bases and 150+ facial morphs; says personal and commercial use allowed. However, the page says the free bases lack realistic skin textures and clothing, and its download link currently displays unavailable. This is not a ready-made photorealistic animated girl.
- A Sketchfab free girl candidate is explicitly a game character, uses a Mixamo-based skeleton, and is CC BY attribution: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/game-character-girl-rigged-textured-animated-e304748a4d704299bb1c37bb6f8cf40e . Useful for a free stylized demo, but does not meet the user's realism requirement.
- Conclusion: Blender/Mixamo are genuinely no-cost. A completely zero-purchase route may be possible through free base meshes plus extensive manual modeling, skin/textures, clothes, rigging, custom action animation, optimization, and licensing checks. No verified ready-to-use free asset was found that meets the user's specified photorealism and custom hand/girl performance, so do not silently replace the requested realism with a cartoon. No application files changed in this follow-up.
